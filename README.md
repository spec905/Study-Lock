# Study-Lock

A web app that helps students plan revison and stay focused Study Lock lets you save multiple exams,build a realistic study schedule,track topics and run timed focus sessions.Everything is saved in the browser,so your plans and history stay even after you close the page

# Quick start

-Open the demo:[ https://spec905.github.io/Study-Lock/](< https://spec905.github.io/Study-Lock/>) -Click Get started -Create an exam (name + future date + optional topics) -Click Save exam -Go to Lock in session, choose the exam, and start a timer -No install needed for the live demo.

# Features

## 1. Exam Planning

- Save multiple exams with their names, dates, and topics.
- Choose how many days per week to study (3–7 days).
- Generate a realistic study schedule with spaced study sessions and review days.
- Check off tasks as you complete them.
- View today's tasks and the number of days remaining until each exam.

## 2. Focus Sessions

- Choose between 30-minute, 60-minute, and open-ended focus sessions.
- Add optional background music using Spotify or YouTube links.
- Save your music link and change it whenever you want.
- Start the timer only when you press **START NOW**, so you don't lose study time while setting up your music.
- Use fullscreen focus mode with a random motivational quote.
- Complete a simple math challenge if you want to end a timed session early.
- Receive a sound notification when a timed session ends.

## 3. Progress Tracking
- Track your study streak.
- Receive milestone messages at 3, 7, 14, and 30 days.
- Set a daily study-time goal in hours and minutes.
- Track your progress with a daily goal bar.
- View your study history, including:
  - Total study time for each exam.
  - Individual sessions with the exam name, date, time, and duration.

# How it works
Scheduling When you save an exam, the app builds a plan from today until the exam date: Study days are spaced out (not every calendar day) Your “days per week” setting limits how dense the plan is Weekdays are preferred when you choose fewer than 7 days Topics are distributed across study days The last 1–2 days are reserved for review / practice test
