# study-Lock
a web app that helps students plan revison and stay focused
# features 
with study-lock you can save exams as much as you want with thr name,date and topics  ,build a realistic study schedule,and run timed sessions. 
.you can choose how many days you want to study per week from 3 to 7
.you can add your goal grade so you can compare it to your actual score,because the grading systems are different from country to another you can choose your grading system
.you can check off taks as you complete and see how many days left until the exam
.the time lock mode is: 30 min or 60 min or an open session
.you can add music as well just by clicking yes music,still optional àd you can change your music player link at any time
.since you can loose some minutes searching for a music ,don't worry the time start automaticly when you click just lock in so if you choose yes to music the time will start only when you click on start now
.a fullscreen lock with random motivation quote 
.if you want to leave early you have to solve a math challenge (an easy when) or just continue your time
.when the session ends you will receive a sound notification 
.you can track your study streak
.every 3,7,14 and 30 days you receive a Milestone messages
.set a daily goal (hours and minutes)
you can track your progress daily with the daily goal bar
.view your study history like total study time for each exam

# how it works
click "get started"to open the main page
add your exam name ,date and how many days per week (you can add a as much as topic if you want)
.click save exams,the app will generate a plan from today until the exam day based on your days per week,leaving the last 2 days for review and ppractice test.if you want to check your progress by grades,it calculates the differnce as a percentage of the mawimum grade , not just points (differnce=goal grade-actual grade. difference percent=(difference/max grade)*100.after comparing ,the app gives you a feedback
